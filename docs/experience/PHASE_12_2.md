# Phase 12.2 — Motion, morphing and microinteractions

## Scope

Promote the existing motion tokens into a semantic financial interaction system.

## Semantics

- Motion intents are named for real product states: navigation, disclosure, privacy, data refresh, transfer reconciliation, investment contribution and modal presentation.
- Full motion preserves spatial continuity where it explains state changes.
- Reduced motion removes transforms and preserves meaning through short opacity transitions.
- Financial values never use count-up animation.
- Continuous decorative loops and hover-only affordances are prohibited.
- Bank-to-investment contribution motion represents relocation of wealth and does not imply spending or loss.
- Privacy transitions are explicitly named and remain available under reduced motion without exposing sensitive values.

## Gate

12.2 closes when semantic motion profiles, web theme mappings, reduced-motion equivalence and regression coverage merge with CI green.
