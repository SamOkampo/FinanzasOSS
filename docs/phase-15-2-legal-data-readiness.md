# Phase 15.2 — Legal and data-treatment readiness

## Internal readiness implemented

The application now requires an explicit external review reference before legal/data readiness can be considered complete. The evidence contract requires references for the privacy notice, retention policy, data-subject-rights procedure, third-party recipient assessment, jurisdiction review and a named human approver with timestamp.

## Boundary

Code cannot approve its own legal review and this repository does not provide a legal conclusion. No legal requirement, retention period, cross-border rule or third-party obligation is invented here.

## External gate still required

A qualified human reviewer must actually perform and approve the applicable legal/privacy review and provide the references represented by this contract. Until then, 15.2 remains unchecked in ROADMAP.
